# Human-Computer Interaction in Healthcare – Exoskeleton Systems Analysis

## Overview

This project explores the role of exoskeleton technology in healthcare through a Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) perspective.
The focus is on understanding how wearable robotic systems interact with the human body to restore mobility, particularly for individuals with physical disabilities.


## Objective

To analyse how exoskeleton systems:

* Support human movement and rehabilitation
* Integrate with human biomechanics
* Enhance user capability through assistive technology


## Background

Exoskeletons are wearable robotic systems designed to augment or restore human physical abilities. Widely used in medical rehabilitation, these systems enable individuals with mobility impairments to perform movements such as walking, standing, and lifting.

This project examines the evolution, structure, and real-world impact of such systems.


## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Human–Machine Interaction

* Direct interaction between human body and robotic system
* Sensor-based motion detection and response
* Adaptive support based on user movement

### 2. System Structure

* Mechanical framework aligned with human anatomy
* Integration of motors and force sensors
* Real-time motion assistance and feedback

### 3. Applications in Healthcare

* Rehabilitation for paralyzed patients
* Mobility assistance for elderly users
* Support for daily physical activities


## Insights

* Exoskeletons represent a significant advancement in assistive technology
* Effective design depends on seamless integration between human and machine
* User comfort, adaptability, and efficiency are critical factors
* These systems highlight the importance of HCI in life-changing technologies


## Future Scope

* Development of lighter, more adaptive exoskeleton systems
* Integration with AI for predictive and personalized movement support
* Improved accessibility and affordability in healthcare
* Expansion into industrial and everyday use cases


## What I Learned

* The importance of designing technology around human needs
* How HCI principles apply to real-world, high-impact systems
* The role of sensors and feedback loops in human-machine interaction
* The potential of combining technology with human capability
